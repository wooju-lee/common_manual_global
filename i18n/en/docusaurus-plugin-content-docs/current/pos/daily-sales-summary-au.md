# 📍 Daily Sales Summary

> Use this menu to check the quantity and amount sold at the store on a selected date, **by product category** and **by product (SKU)**.
>
> Path : <span className="path-badge">IIC BO > FRONT POS > Daily Sales Summary</span>

## 👉 When to use

- To check **how much of each product category was sold** at the store today (or on a specific date)
- To quickly check **sales quantity and amount** by product

## 1. Look Up Sales

<div className="img-placeholder">📷 [Image] Daily Sales Summary screen</div>

Select one date to look up in **SALES DATE**. When you open the menu, it shows **today's date** by default.

<div className="blockquote-gray">

> A POS account has access to **only 1 store**, so only data for the logged-in account's store is shown. You can check the current store at the top left of the screen.

</div>

## 2. What You Can See on the Screen

| Area | Description |
|---|---|
| **SALES VIEW - PRODUCT CATEGORY 1 (MODEL)** | Sales quantity and amount by product category (e.g. `OPTICAL`, `PACKAGE`) |
| **SALES VIEW - SKU** | Sales quantity and amount by product |

- In the product category area, **Total (Exclude package)** is the total excluding packages, and **Total** is the grand total including packages.
- **Packages** included with products are excluded from the sales quantity, so their quantity and amount are shown as `0`.
